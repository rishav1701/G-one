# Data Provenance

All application patient and observation rows used by G One are synthetic demonstration data. The deterministic simulator creates 12 synthetic admissions in two simulation wards across six scripted system-behaviour scenarios. Patient identifiers use the `SIM-` prefix; the simulator labels measurements as simulated and submits them through the normal API. Values come from deterministic scenario code, not from the PhysioNet training files. Manually entered observations are new user-entered records on these synthetic patients; neither the simulator nor manual entries train a model. Seed results are reproducible for a fixed seed.

Synthetic labs are read-only demonstration values. They are excluded from NEWS2 and model features. Scenario counts, alert rates, and simulator outcomes describe software behaviour, not clinical performance or outcomes.

The ward-oriented ML feature contract remains fixture-tested. Separately, a research candidate was trained from the public PhysioNet Challenge 2019 v1.0.0 ICU sepsis data under CC BY 4.0. Its attributed model and aggregate metrics are packaged at `models/research/physionet-challenge-2019-sepsis`; it is not calibrated, not validated for wards or NEWS2, and is not in `models/active`. Full downloaded patient-level files remain in ignored `.tools/physionet-challenge-2019` and are not committed. No credentialed data was accessed.

Audit and operational records are generated by application actions in the local demonstration database. The simulator's reset command clears and recreates its synthetic patients, admissions, observations, alerts, and source states while retaining its two named ward records so assigned demo users keep access; it does not create or read real patient data.
