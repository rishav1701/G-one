# Data Provenance

All application patient and observation rows are synthetic demonstration data. The deterministic simulator creates 12 synthetic admissions in two simulation wards across six scripted system-behaviour scenarios. Patient identifiers use the `SIM-` prefix; the simulator labels synthetic measurements as simulated and submits them through the normal API. Seed results are reproducible for a fixed seed.

Synthetic labs are demonstration values and are read-only. They are excluded from NEWS2 and model features. Scenario counts, alert rates, and simulator outcomes describe this software's scripted behavior; they are not clinical performance, outcomes, or validation evidence.

The ML training pipeline currently uses generated fixtures only. No credentialed patient-level dataset was read or included. The M0 data decision record remains unfilled; an approved dataset, cohort definition, outcome/horizon, and acceptance criteria are required before any locally authorized training. No trained model is deployed.

Audit and operational records are generated by application actions in the local demonstration database. The simulator's reset command clears and recreates its synthetic dataset; it does not create or read real patient data.
